Clean facilities · Shared responsibility

Monitoring of Cleanliness
of Grade 11 Building CR

A school monitoring system for recording CR users, weekly cleaning assignments, cleanliness inspections, facility concerns, feedback, and maintenance trends.

What is this website for?

This website helps the school community keep organized records of CR use and cleaning duties. It makes it easier to identify missed monitoring, follow up on reported problems, check whether assigned cleaning routines are being completed, and notice recurring maintenance concerns. Records are for verification and fair follow-up; a record alone does not automatically prove that a person or section is at fault.

Hygiene awareness

A clean CR protects everyone.

A clean comfort room helps reduce unpleasant odors, limits the spread of germs, and gives every student a safer and more comfortable school environment. Keeping it clean is everyone's responsibility.

💧 Pour water into the toilet bowl after use.
There is no flush system, so make sure to pour enough water.
🌸 Keep the restroom fresh and odor-free.
Use only school-approved and safe cleaning or deodorizing products placed in the toilet bowl.
🗑️ Dispose of waste in the proper bin.
Do not leave tissue or litter on the floor.
🧼 Wash your hands.
Use soap and clean water after using the restroom.
🧹 Keep the surrounding area clean.
Do not leave dirty water or litter on the floor.
🤝 Take care of the facilities.
Use the faucet, door, pail, and other facilities properly.
📢 Report any problem.
Report water shortages, damaged facilities, or missing cleaning supplies.
✨ Think of the next user.
Leave the restroom clean and ready for the next person.
Reminder: Never mix cleaning chemicals or use products that have not been approved by the school.
